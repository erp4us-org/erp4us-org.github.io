An organization should be able to change how its business software runs without losing access to its own records. That is a design constraint, not a feature we can declare finished. In erp4us it means avoiding dependencies on a single cloud or AI provider and making service interfaces inspectable.

The [first slice](https://github.com/erp4us-org/erp4us#run-the-first-slice) only registers and retrieves an article in a local environment. It helps test a small part of the design without pretending to cover ERP workflows. The next question is whether those boundaries still hold for an order that needs several services and a decision by a buyer.

## A useful first contribution

Bring a fictional workflow from your industry. Tell us which records need to move between teams, what must stay private and who can correct a mistake. [Share it in GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/erp4us-org/discussions/72).
